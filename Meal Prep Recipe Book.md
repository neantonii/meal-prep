#### **Instructions**

5. **Portion:** Transfer the single portion (205 g cooked) directly to a meal prep container.

#### **Instructions**

1. **Thaw and Dry:** Place 227 g shrimp in a colander and run cold water over them for 3 to 4 minutes until thawed. Pat completely dry on all sides using paper towels (removing surface moisture is essential to sear rather than steam).  
2. **Season Dry:** In a small bowl, toss the dried shrimp with kosher salt, coarse black pepper, and smoked paprika.  
3. **High-Heat Sear:** Place a skillet over medium-high heat until hot. Lay shrimp in a single flat layer in the dry skillet. Sear undisturbed for 90 seconds on side one. Flip and sear for 30 to 45 seconds on side two.

5. **Portion:** Transfer immediately to a meal prep container (175 g cooked yield) and pour any remaining lemon-butter juices over the top.
